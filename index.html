HOW TO PLAY

You're on defense. Stop the offense on 4 drives.

1. Read it. Before every snap a call or rules question pops up. Watch the formation and the motion. Answer before the play clock runs out.

2. Right answer = great jump. You start fast and the runner slows down. Wrong answer = you're out of position and the runner breaks free.

3. Make the tackle. Touch and drag on the field (or use arrow keys) to move your player, the one with the YOU arrow. Tap DIVE (or space) for a burst and a longer reach. Dive tackles can force fumbles.

4. Get off the field. Hold them on 3 downs to force a punt. Inside your 35 they'll kick a field goal.

Points: correct answer 100 + speed bonus + streak bonus, tackle 50, tackle for loss 150, stop 500, turnover 750.
